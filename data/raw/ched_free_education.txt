MORE FILIPINO STUDENTS CAN AVAIL FREE HIGHER EDUCATION WITH 27 NEWLY CHED-RECOGNIZED LUCs

The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) has recognized 27 new Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs) nationwide, expanding access to free higher education under Republic Act No. 10931 or the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act.

WHAT IS RA 10931?
Republic Act No. 10931, signed into law on August 3, 2017, provides free tuition and exemption from other fees in state universities and colleges (SUCs), local universities and colleges (LUCs), and state-run technical-vocational institutions.

KEY PROVISIONS:
1. Free Tuition: All Filipino students enrolled in SUCs and CHED-recognized LUCs are exempt from tuition fees.
2. Free Miscellaneous Fees: Students from families with a combined annual gross income of Php 400,000 or below are exempt from miscellaneous and other school fees.
3. Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES): Eligible students receive financial assistance for costs of education such as books, supplies, transportation, and personal expenses.

TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE AS A RECOGNIZED LUC:
Talibon Polytechnic College (TPC) is among the CHED-recognized Local Universities and Colleges. As a recognized LUC, TPC students are entitled to:
- Free tuition fees
- Exemption from miscellaneous fees (for qualifying students)
- Access to the Tertiary Education Subsidy program

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
To avail of free higher education benefits, students must:
- Be a Filipino citizen
- Be enrolled in a CHED-recognized program
- Not be a recipient of other government-funded scholarships covering tuition
- Meet the income threshold for miscellaneous fee exemption (Php 400,000 combined family annual gross income)

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Enroll in any CHED-recognized program at TPC
2. Submit required documents to the Registrar's Office
3. Complete the Student Assessment Form
4. For TES application, submit additional financial documents

For more information, contact the CHED Regional Office or the TPC Registrar's Office.
