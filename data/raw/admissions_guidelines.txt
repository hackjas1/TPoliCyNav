TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE (TPC)
OFFICIAL ADMISSIONS, ENTRANCE EXAM (TPCAT), AND DEGREE PROGRAMS GUIDELINES
Source: TPC Admissions Office & TPC Student Handbook v.04 (Chapter 10, Page 157)

================================================================================
PART 1: OFFICIAL DEGREE PROGRAMS AND COURSES OFFERED AT TPC
================================================================================
Talibon Polytechnic College (TPC) in Bohol officially offers the following 7 undergraduate degree programs:

1. Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS) - 4-Year Program
2. Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSCrim) - 4-Year Program
3. Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems (BSAIS) - 4-Year Program
4. Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (BSAgri) - 4-Year Program (with Diploma in Agriculture Technology exit path after 2 years)
5. Bachelor of Arts in English Language (BAEL) - 4-Year Program
6. Bachelor of Arts in Political Science (BAPoS) - 4-Year Program
7. Bachelor of Early Childhood Education (BECEd) - 4-Year Program

All degree programs are recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and covered under Republic Act No. 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act / Free Tuition Policy).

================================================================================
PART 2: TPC COLLEGE ADMISSION TEST (TPCAT) & REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
Aspiring freshmen and transferees must undergo the TPC College Admission Test (TPCAT).
Requirements for TPCAT Application:
1. Duly accomplished TPCAT Application Form
2. Certified True Copy of Form 138 (Senior High School Report Card) / Transcript of Records for Transferees
3. Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school
4. 2 copies of 2x2 ID picture with white background
5. Photocopy of PSA Birth Certificate

================================================================================
PART 3: ENTRANCE EXAM DAY REMINDERS & DRESS CODE
================================================================================
Examinees must observe the following rules during the exam:
1. Bring official Examination Permit and valid ID.
2. Bring pencils (No. 2), black ballpens, and eraser.
3. Dress Code: Examinees must wear decent, respectful attire (plain white shirt or polo, decent pants/jeans, and closed shoes).
4. Strictly PROHIBITED on exam day: Ripped/tattered jeans, short shorts, sleeveless shirts, sando, crop tops, open slippers/sandals, and unauthorized electronic devices.
