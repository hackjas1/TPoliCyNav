GSIS SUBSIDY FOR SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING, AND MATHEMATICS (GSSP)

The Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) provides financial assistance to qualified dependents of GSIS members through the GSIS Subsidy for STEM Program (GSSP).

PROGRAM OVERVIEW:
The GSSP aims to support the education of dependents of GSIS members who are pursuing Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) courses in recognized higher education institutions.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
1. The applicant must be a dependent (child) of an active or retired GSIS member.
2. The applicant must be enrolled in a STEM-related course in a CHED-recognized institution.
3. The applicant must maintain a General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 90% or its equivalent.
4. The applicant must not be more than 21 years old at the time of application.
5. The applicant must not be a recipient of any other government scholarship grant.

BENEFITS:
- Annual subsidy for tuition and other school fees
- Book allowance
- Monthly stipend

APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS:
1. Accomplished GSSP Application Form
2. Certificate of Enrollment in a STEM course
3. Certified True Copy of Grades (with at least 90% GWA)
4. Birth Certificate (PSA)
5. GSIS Member's Service Record or Certification of Membership
6. 2 pcs. 2x2 ID Photo

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Download the GSSP Application Form from the GSIS website (www.gsis.gov.ph)
2. Submit the accomplished form and requirements to the nearest GSIS branch
3. Wait for the evaluation and approval of your application
4. Once approved, the subsidy will be released directly to the student's school

RENEWAL:
- The subsidy is renewable annually, subject to the student maintaining the required GWA
- Students must submit updated grades and enrollment certificate for renewal

For more information, visit the nearest GSIS branch or contact GSIS through their official website at www.gsis.gov.ph.
