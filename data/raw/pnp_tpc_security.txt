PNP AND TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE COLLABORATE TO STRENGTHEN STUDENT SAFETY

The Philippine National Police (PNP) Talibon Municipal Police Station and Talibon Polytechnic College (TPC) have joined forces to strengthen student safety through collaborative security initiatives.

JOINT SAFETY INITIATIVES:

1. BOARDING HOUSE MONITORING
The PNP, in coordination with TPC and the local government, conducts regular inspections of boarding houses, apartments, and dormitories near the campus. These inspections ensure compliance with Municipal Ordinance No. 127, which regulates the establishment and operation of student accommodations near TPC.

Key monitoring activities include:
- Verification of boarding house registration and permits
- Inspection of mandatory CCTV installations
- Review of logbook records for student check-in/check-out
- Assessment of fire safety equipment and emergency exits
- Verification of compliance with curfew regulations

2. CURFEW ENFORCEMENT
The PNP enforces curfew regulations for students residing in boarding houses near the campus. Students are required to be in their respective accommodations by the designated curfew time. Violations are reported to both the boarding house operator and the TPC administration.

3. ALCOHOL AND ENTERTAINMENT PROHIBITION ZONE
In compliance with Municipal Ordinance No. 127, a strict 500-meter prohibition zone around the TPC campus has been established. Within this zone:
- No establishment shall sell alcoholic beverages
- No entertainment establishments (karaoke bars, billiard halls) are permitted
- Violations result in fines and potential closure of the establishment

4. CAMPUS SECURITY COORDINATION
The PNP works closely with the TPC Chief Security Officer to:
- Conduct security briefings for students during orientation
- Provide rapid response for campus emergencies
- Assist in enforcing the college's discipline policies when needed
- Support anti-drug awareness campaigns on campus

5. STUDENT SAFETY HOTLINE
A dedicated hotline has been established for students to report safety concerns, including:
- Harassment or bullying incidents
- Boarding house violations
- Security threats
- Emergency situations

The collaboration between PNP and TPC aims to create a safe and conducive learning environment for the approximately 4,900 students enrolled at the college. Both institutions are committed to the holistic development and protection of the student body.

This initiative is part of the broader safety framework implemented by the Local Government Unit of Talibon under the leadership of the Municipal Mayor.
