TALIBON MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 127
Series of 2020 / Revised Provisions

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE ESTABLISHMENT, MANAGEMENT, AND OPERATION OF APARTMENTS, BOARDING HOUSES, HOMESTAYS, AND DORMITORIES NEAR TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE (TPC), PRESCRIBING SAFETY STANDARDS, SECURITY PROTOCOLS, CURFEW HOURS, AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF.

SECTION 1. TITLE
This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "Talibon Municipal Ordinance No. 127: TPC Student Boarding and Housing Regulatory Act."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY
It is the policy of the Municipal Government of Talibon to safeguard the welfare, security, moral well-being, and academic focus of students residing in boarding houses, dormitories, homestays, and apartments within the municipality, particularly those enrolled in Talibon Polytechnic College (TPC).

SECTION 3. COVERAGE AND SCOPE
This Ordinance covers all natural or juridical entities operating, managing, or maintaining apartments, boarding houses, dormitories, and homestay facilities within the Municipality of Talibon catering to students of Talibon Polytechnic College, especially those located within a 1-kilometer radius of the TPC campus.

SECTION 4. MANDATORY PERMITS AND LICENSING
No person or entity shall operate a student boarding house, apartment, or dormitory without securing the following prerequisite clearances and permits:
1. Valid Mayor's Business Permit from the LGU of Talibon.
2. Sanitary Permit and Health Certificates from the Municipal Health Office.
3. Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP).
4. Zoning Clearance and Building Inspection Approval.
5. Registration Certificate and Joint Compliance Accreditation from the TPC Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS).

SECTION 5. PHYSICAL AND SECURITY STANDARDS
Every covered establishment must comply with the following structural and security requirements:
1. Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV): Mandatory installation of high-definition CCTV cameras covering all main entrance doors, common corridors, and perimeter gates, with continuous recording retained for at least thirty (30) days.
2. Fire Safety Measures: Accessible, fully charged fire extinguishers on every floor, clearly marked emergency exit routes, functioning smoke detectors, and emergency lighting.
3. Adequate Sanitation and Space: Clean drinking water facilities, adequate toilet-to-student ratios (at least 1 toilet/bath per 5 boarders), proper ventilation, and well-lit study areas.
4. Gender Segregation: Boarding houses offering mixed-gender accommodations must maintain strictly separated wings, floors, or quarters with distinct access controls.

SECTION 6. CURFEW HOURS AND LOGBOOK MONITORING
1. Mandatory Curfew: A standard curfew of 10:00 PM is hereby enforced for all student residents from Sunday through Thursday, and 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday, except when official academic activities or school-authorized events are certified in writing by the TPC Administration.
2. Official Tenant and Visitor Logbook: Operators must maintain a daily logbook recording tenant entry and exit times, visitor identities, time in, time out, and purpose of visit. Overnight visitors of the opposite sex are strictly prohibited unless authorized by the tenant's parents or legal guardians in writing.
3. Compliance Reporting: Operators are required to report chronic absenteeism, unauthorized curfew violations, or suspicious activities of student boarders to the TPC Dean of Student Discipline and the PNP Talibon Station.

SECTION 7. 500-METER ALCOHOL AND ENTERTAINMENT PROHIBITION ZONE
In strict compliance with municipal health and security guidelines:
1. Alcohol Prohibition: No store, eatery, convenience shop, or establishment located within five hundred (500) meters from the perimeter fence of Talibon Polytechnic College shall sell, serve, or distribute intoxicating liquor, beer, wine, or any alcoholic beverage to students.
2. Entertainment Establishments: Karaoke bars, videoke joints, billiard halls, and internet gaming cafes within the 500-meter zone are prohibited from operating during official class hours (7:30 AM to 5:30 PM) and must cease operations by 10:00 PM.
3. Illegal Substances: Possession, distribution, or use of illegal drugs, electronic cigarettes/vapes, or tobacco within boarding premises is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate police intervention.

SECTION 8. JOINT MONITORING AND INSPECTION TEAM
A Joint Monitoring Team composed of representatives from:
1. The Philippine National Police (PNP) Talibon Station
2. The Municipal Health and Sanitation Office
3. The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)
4. The TPC Chief Security Officer and OSAS Representative
5. The Barangay Officials of the host barangay
shall conduct regular unannounced monthly inspections to ensure full compliance with the provisions of this Ordinance.

SECTION 9. PENALTIES AND SANCTIONS
Violations by operators of boarding houses, apartments, or commercial establishments shall be penalized as follows:
- First Offense: Official Written Warning and a fine of One Thousand Pesos (Php 1,000.00), with a 15-day rectification notice.
- Second Offense: A fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (Php 2,500.00) and temporary suspension of Business Permit until full compliance.
- Third and Subsequent Offenses: A fine of Five Thousand Pesos (Php 5,000.00), permanent revocation of Mayor's Business Permit, and immediate padlocking/closure of the establishment.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval and publication in accordance with the Local Government Code of the Philippines.
