TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE (TPC)
OFFICIAL SCHOOL UNIFORM WEARING GUIDELINES & PROPER DRESS CODE POLICY
Source: TPC Student Handbook v.04 (Pages 106-107, 112, 196-200) & Institutional Policy

================================================================================
PART 1: GENERAL COLLEGE SCHOOL UNIFORM — WEARING RULES & INQUIRIES
================================================================================
The official TPC school uniform is mandatory on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays. The college strictly enforces a "NO ID / NO UNIFORM, NO ENTRY" policy.

1. PROPER WEARING GUIDELINES FOR FEMALE STUDENTS:
- Blouse: Official TPC Type-A blouse (worn untucked / not tucked in).
- Skirt / Pants: Official TPC school skirt, which must be knee-level in length (skirts above the knee or micro-minis are strictly prohibited). Alternatively, official college uniform pants may be worn.
- Shoes: Black closed formal shoes worn with socks (sandals, slippers, open-toe shoes, and crocs are strictly prohibited).
- Identification: Official TPC School ID visibly displayed at all times around the neck on the official lanyard.

2. PROPER WEARING GUIDELINES FOR MALE STUDENTS:
- Polo: Official TPC Type-A polo shirt, worn with an inner plain white sleeveless shirt (sando).
- Pants: Official college uniform pants/slacks.
- Shoes: Black closed formal shoes worn with socks.
- Identification: Official TPC School ID visibly displayed at all times around the neck on the official lanyard.

3. WHERE TO INQUIRE FOR OFFICIAL UNIFORM DESIGNS AND TAILORING:
- For official fabric specifications, color shades, patches, and tailoring patterns for the general college uniform, students should go directly to the **TPC Registrar's Office** or the **Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS)** for official information and accredited tailoring coordination.

================================================================================
PART 2: GUIDELINES FOR FIRST-YEAR / NEW STUDENTS (WITHOUT UNIFORMS)
================================================================================
For incoming first-year freshmen and new transferee students who do not yet possess the official school uniform:
- Allowed Attire: First-year students waiting for their official uniforms are permitted to wear:
  1. Plain white T-shirt (clean, decent, without inappropriate prints or graphics).
  2. Long pants (formal slacks or decent jeans; tattered or ripped jeans are strictly not allowed).
  3. Closed shoes with socks (rubber shoes, sneakers, or casual closed shoes; slippers and open sandals are prohibited).
- Temporary Identification: Students waiting for their official ID card must present their Certificate of Registration (COR) or temporary pass issued by the Registrar at the campus entrance gate.
- Transition Grace Period: Freshmen are granted a transition period at the beginning of the semester to have their official uniforms tailored.

================================================================================
PART 3: CRIMINOLOGY DEPARTMENT SPECIALIZED UNIFORMS (TYPES A, B, C)
================================================================================
The College of Criminology implements specialized uniforms for different training, academic, and ceremonial activities:

1. CRIMINOLOGY TYPE A & TYPE B UNIFORMS:
- For exact design guidelines, official colors, shoulder boards/epaulets, badges, patches, and tailoring specifications for Criminology Type A (Formal/Inspection) and Type B (Daily Classroom/Semi-Formal), students must consult the **College of Criminology Department Head / Dean's Office** or the **TPC Registrar's Office** for the latest approved institutional standards.

2. CRIMINOLOGY TYPE C UNIFORM (Physical Training / Tactical Day):
- Plain solid white round-neck T-shirt (tucked in).
- Dark pants / tactical trousers with belt.
- Clean white rubber shoes / sneakers with white socks.
- Official TPC ID displayed on chest.

================================================================================
PART 4: PROPER DRESS CODE & PROHIBITED IMPROPER ATTIRE (PAGE 200)
================================================================================
On wash days, civilian days, entrance examinations, or when visiting campus, all students must strictly adhere to the College Dress Code. The following 12 items are STRICTLY PROHIBITED on campus:

PROHIBITED UPPER ATTIRE:
1. Sleeveless tops, tank tops, and sando worn as outer clothing.
2. Backless tops or dresses.
3. Tube tops (strapless).
4. Off-shoulder tops (exposing bare shoulders).
5. Crop tops (exposing the midriff or navel).

PROHIBITED LOWER ATTIRE:
6. Shorts of any kind (denim shorts, cycling shorts, athletic shorts, cargo shorts).
7. Micro mini-skirts (skirts significantly above knee level).
8. Tattered, ripped, or distressed jeans/skirts (pants with tears, holes, or frayed patches).

PROHIBITED FOOTWEAR:
9. Slippers / flip-flops (rubber slippers / tsinelas).
10. Crocs / rubber clogs.
11. Casual open sandals / strapped plastic sandals.
12. Open shoes (peep-toe heels, open-toe footwear).

UNIFORM EXEMPTION & DISCIPLINARY MATRICES:
- Uniform Exemption Pass: Issued by OSAS to (a) pregnant students, (b) cross-enrollees, (c) religious orders, and (d) documented medical reasons.
- Violations: Disciplinary matrix classifies uniform/dress code violations as Minor Offenses subject to formative interventions (Warning, Reprimand, Apology).
